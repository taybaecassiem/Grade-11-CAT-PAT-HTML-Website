What is E-waste?

E-waste is any electronic device or piece of electrical equipment which has been thrown away and these items can be working or broken.
E-waste includes all electronic devices which require electricity or batteries to work.


The Eight Different Types of E-waste Are:

  • Appliances and Power Tools: common household appliances, outdoor tools, and power tools
  • Speakers and Sound Equipment
  • Medical Equipment
  • Lighting
  • Batteries, Wiring and Switches
  • Home Heating and Cooling
  • Solar Power Systems
  • Toys and Gadgets
